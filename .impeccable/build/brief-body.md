OWN-WORLD: Near-black #111111/#191818 ground; mint #7AF298 as the only colour; white Space Grotesk, uppercase and bold for display; code punctuation as ornament (`//` eyebrows, `</ … >` framed rules, `/` link prefixes, `E`/`T` letter labels); pill tags in mint on translucent grey; thin #505559 rules.
STORY: In one screen the visitor learns who he is (the name), what he does ("Software Engineer & AI Developer", AI that helps people learn) and how to reach him (E/T rows, socials, Start Project). The scroll then proves it: skills and stats, the three case studies, services, process, background, contact.
