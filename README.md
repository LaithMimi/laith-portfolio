## 5. Case Studies

### GeeksHub (Capstone Project)
- **Tagline**: "Where university chaos becomes organized learning"
- **Problem**: University students juggle scattered resources — PDFs across WhatsApp groups, lecture notes lost in email threads, exam materials buried in course portals.
- **Solution**: Centralizes all academic resources into one intelligent hub with RAG-powered AI assistant and gamification.
- **Impact**: Reduced material search time from hours to seconds. Students report clearer study sessions and less pre-exam panic. (Metrics TBD)
- **Tech Stack**: React, Python, FastAPI, RAG Workflows, Vector DB
- **Screenshots**: Dashboard, AI assistant, gamification leaderboard, mobile view

### Arabic Chatbot AI Tutor
- **Tagline**: "Your patient, always-available Arabic teacher"
- **Problem**: Learning Arabic is difficult — complex grammar, dialect variations, limited access to native speakers.
- **Solution**: RAG-based conversational tutor that adapts to learner's level with Firebase progress tracking and Pinecone semantic search.
- **Impact**: Learners practice without judgment or scheduling constraints. (Metrics TBD)
- **Tech Stack**: React, Python, Firebase, Pinecone, RAG
- **Screenshots**: Chat interface, grammar explanations, progress tracking, vocabulary review

### NGO Social Platform
- **Tagline**: "Connecting communities through shared purpose"
- **Problem**: Local NGO needed to engage community online but lacked technical resources.
- **Solution**: Social platform with community features + compelling landing page. Firebase powers real-time interactions.
- **Impact**: Improved clarity and accessibility for community engagement. (Metrics TBD)
- **Tech Stack**: React, Firebase, Responsive Design
- **Screenshots**: Landing page, community feed, member profiles, event registration
